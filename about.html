05 • ABOUT SCANBEE

Technology that makes the honey chain easier to understand.

ScanBee is a hackathon prototype focused on the digital experience around honey traceability and smart beekeeping management.

Connect the people, data and stages.

The concept combines a traceable batch identity, hive-level monitoring and a consumer verification experience.

For this demo, values are simulated. A production system would require real sensors, authenticated records, a database and an appropriate blockchain architecture.

ScanBee pillars

✓ Honey batch traceability

✓ Smart hive monitoring

✓ Quality information

✓ Consumer verification
