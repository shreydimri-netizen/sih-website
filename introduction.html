01 • INTRODUCTION

A connected journey for every honey batch.

ScanBee is a prototype platform for honey traceability and smart beekeeping management. It demonstrates how hive data can become useful information for everyone along the honey chain.

THE IDEA

From scattered records to one connected story.

Honey passes through several stages before reaching a consumer. ScanBee provides a digital structure for recording hive information, harvest details, quality checks and packaging references.

🐝 ScanBee in one line

Scan a batch. See its journey. Understand the data.

Four simple stages

01 — Hive

Capture hive and environmental information.

02 — Harvest

Create a traceable batch when honey is collected.

03 — Quality

Associate inspection information with the batch.

04 — Consumer

Verify the available journey using a batch reference.
