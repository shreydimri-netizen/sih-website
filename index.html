Why this matters

Honeybees pollinate a large share of the crops we eat. Colony Collapse Disorder and hive stress often show early warning signs in temperature, humidity, and activity patterns — long before a beekeeper would notice by eye. A simple monitoring dashboard like this one helps catch problems early.
